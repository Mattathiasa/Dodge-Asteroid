How to play

Stay alive. Everything below is how you score while you do it.

  • Near misses build your combo. Skim a rock without touching it. The ring round your ship is the time left to land the next one.

  • Star shards are points. Fly through them; they are pulled in when you are close, and your combo multiplies them.

  • A red lane means a comet. It falls straight down that lane a moment later, fast. Get out of it.

  • Shield — vaporises the first thing it touches, for six seconds.

  • Slow-mo — the field slows for five seconds. You do not.

  • Extra life — survive one more hit, up to four.

Every 25 seconds you cross into a new sector, and the field changes colour with it.

About

Dodge Asteroid is a small arcade game: stay alive as the field fills up. Score comes from time survived, rocks dodged and star shards collected, and threading a near miss builds a combo that multiplies all of it.

It is written in TypeScript and rendered with the Canvas 2D API, with no runtime dependencies. The simulation runs on a fixed timestep, so it behaves identically at any frame rate, and every random value comes from a seeded generator, which makes a whole run reproducible in tests. The music and every sound are synthesised as you play.

Controls — move with the mouse, touch, arrow keys or WASD. P or Esc pauses, R restarts, M mutes.
